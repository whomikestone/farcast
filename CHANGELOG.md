# Changelog

Every change to Farcast, newest first. One line per day (Pacific time); changes on the same day are separated by semicolons, in the order they were made.
This file is also shown in the app at `/changelog` ("What's new").

2026.11.01 — The hand magnify effect now starts only when your pointer reaches your hand, so the bottom of your battlefield is usable again; much larger card preview: it now appears over the sidebar while you point at a card (sizes S, M, and L; L reaches onto the table); token stacks: making several tokens at once makes one card with a count (×5) that you can add to, take from, edit, or split; edits in the Edit token window are no longer lost when the other player acts; turn numbers count rounds: both players play turn 1, and the number goes up only when the player who went first starts their turn again; your own hand count now shows next to your library count, like your opponent's; choose how the card preview appears (View…): pop up while you point at a card, or pinned in the top of the sidebar until you point at another
2026.10.30 — Practice against a bot: a simple opponent with a Commander deck that plays a land, casts what it can, attacks, and passes; games against it are always unranked
2026.10.17 — Reveal cards to your opponent from the top of your library or your hand; put the rest of the cards you looked at on the bottom, in a random or chosen order; additional starting life (20) in the lobby; card size settings for your hand and the table; larger card preview, with a size setting; counters up to 999; magnify effect for cards in hand; massive updates to token card
2026.10.12 — Added the changelog page; updates to playmat area; names can't be updated once chosen; Library updates: filter library searches by card type, close a library search without shuffling, look at the top card of library with one click; mark a card as "doesn't untap"
2026.10.11 — App renamed to Farcast; added unranked games; explicit sign-in, sign-up, and welcome screen URLs; invite links survive signing in; Library updates: put cards anywhere in your library, easier library search; 
2026.10.04 — First playable version: two-player tabletop with hidden hands, commander playmats, saved decks (Commander + Deck boxes), sign-in, and head-to-head records.
