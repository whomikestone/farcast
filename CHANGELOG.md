# Changelog

Every change to Farcast, newest first. One line per day (Pacific time); changes on the same day are separated by semicolons, in the order they were made.
This file is also shown in the app at `/changelog` ("What's new").

2026.10.17 — Reveal cards to your opponent from the top of your library or your hand; put the rest of the cards you looked at on the bottom, in a random or chosen order; change starting life in the lobby; card size settings for your hand and the table; larger card preview, with a size setting; counters up to 999; cards in your hand magnify as you move across them, like the Mac Dock; edit a token's name, power/toughness, type, and counters after creating it; set any card's power/toughness when it's pumped or shrunk
2026.10.12 — Added the changelog page; updates to playmat area; names can't be updated once chosen; Library updates: filter library searches by card type, close a library search without shuffling, look at the top card of library with one click; mark a card as "doesn't untap"
2026.10.11 — App renamed to Farcast; added unranked games; explicit sign-in, sign-up, and welcome screen URLs; invite links survive signing in; Library updates: put cards anywhere in your library, easier library search; 
2026.10.04 — First playable version: two-player tabletop with hidden hands, commander playmats, saved decks (Commander + Deck boxes), sign-in, and head-to-head records.
